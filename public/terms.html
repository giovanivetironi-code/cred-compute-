2. Credits are a prepaid utility, not an investment

  • Credits, including card-purchased account credits and on-chain CRED tokens, can only be used to pay for services on ComputeCred.
  • Credits are not money, securities, deposits or investments. They pay no interest, yield or profit, and we make no promise that their price or value will rise.
  • Credits can't be transferred, sold or exchanged, and they can't be redeemed for cash, except where the law requires it.
  • Testnet CRED on Base Sepolia is for testing only and has no monetary value.

3. Purchases and refunds

  • Card payments are processed by Stripe. Prices are in US dollars, and any applicable taxes may be added.
  • Credits don't expire while the service operates.
  • Credits are non-refundable once used. If a run fails because of an error on our side or our provider's, the credits for that run are returned to your balance automatically.
  • Unused purchased credits are refunded only at our discretion or where required by law. To ask, email support@computecred.ai (or message @ComputeCred on X).
  • We may reverse credits obtained through fraud, chargebacks or abuse.

3a. Credits are closed-loop store credit

  • Credits, including gifted credits and credits earned on the marketplace, are prepaid store credit that can only be used for services on ComputeCred. They have no cash value and can't be withdrawn, sold, exchanged for money or redeemed for cash, except where the law requires it.

3b. Gifts

  • You can send 1 to 25 credits from your balance to an email address, up to 5 gifts and 50 credits per day. The credits leave your balance when you send the gift.
  • Only someone signed in with the recipient email can claim a gift. You can cancel an unclaimed gift, and unclaimed gifts return to your balance after 30 days.
  • Don't use gifts to send spam, harass people or move value obtained by fraud. Gifting to X handles isn't available yet.

3c. Prompt marketplace

  • You can publish prompts and workflows. You confirm you have the right to share them, and they must follow the acceptable use rules below. Your prompt text is kept private and is not shown to other users, though AI output may reflect it.
  • You may add a markup of up to 0.5 credits per run. When someone else runs your prompt, you receive 70% of the markup as ComputeCred credits, under the same store-credit terms. There are no cash payouts.
  • Prompts pass an automatic content filter, can be reported by users, and are hidden automatically after 3 reports. We may hide or remove any prompt and reverse earnings obtained through abuse, including running your own prompts through other accounts.
