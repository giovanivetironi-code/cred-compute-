How it works

FAQ

Questions

What is CRED?

A prepaid credit for AI compute. One CRED buys $1 of AI services on ComputeCred.

Can I trade or transfer CRED?

Not right now. Credits can't be transferred and are only used to pay for compute. Trading will only be considered after a legal review.

Is this an investment?

No. The price is fixed at $1 per credit, and there are no promises of profit, yield or a rising price.

What happens if a run fails?

The backend automatically refunds the credits you spent on that run.

Is this live with real money?

Not yet. ComputeCred is running on the Base Sepolia testnet, and test credits are free.

Which AI powers the services?

xAI's Grok models, with more providers planned.
