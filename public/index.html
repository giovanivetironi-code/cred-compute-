ComputeCred

Prepaid credits for AI agent runs.

Buy CRED at a fixed price of 1 CRED = $1 of compute, then spend it on AI services: chat, writing, research, coding, images, and a math lab. Each spend burns credits on-chain, and our backend runs your job.

⚡ Pay per run

Spending burns your credits and records a run ID. That event triggers the job.

🔒 Hard cap: 23,000,000

No more than 23M CRED ($23M of compute) can ever be minted. Burned credits don't free up room to mint again.

🚫 Non-transferable

Credits can't be sent or traded. They're for buying compute, like a prepaid gift card.

📄 Not an investment

Fixed price: $1 per credit. No profit, yield, or price-increase promises.
